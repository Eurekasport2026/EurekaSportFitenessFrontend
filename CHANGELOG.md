### Added
- **Localized Course Datasets (IT & EN) & Dynamic API Layer**:
  - Separated course datasets into dedicated language directories:
    - `src/lib/api/data/it/`: Italian courses, modules, lessons, exams, and practical resources.
    - `src/lib/api/data/en/`: Authentic English courses, modules, lessons, exams (30 True/False questions for all 5 courses), and practical gym resources extracted directly from curriculum source documents (`Documentation/API/`).
  - Enhanced Mock API Layer (`src/lib/api/courses.ts`, `modules.ts`, `lessons.ts`, `exams.ts`, `practical.ts`) to accept an optional `locale?: string` parameter (defaulting to `"it"` for seamless backward compatibility) that resolves the corresponding language dataset dynamically.
  - Connected `locale` through Next.js App Router dynamic routes: `src/app/[locale]/academy/corsi/[slug]/page.tsx`, `learn/page.tsx`, and `learn/[moduleId]/page.tsx`.
  - Localized client components `PracticalResources.tsx`, `ExamPlaceholder.tsx`, `CourseHome.tsx`, `CourseCompletion.tsx`, and `LessonView.tsx` with dynamic `useLocale()` support.
- **Complete Course LMS Platform & Architecture**:
  - Implemented type-safe Mock API Layer in `src/lib/api/` (`types.ts`, `courses.ts`, `modules.ts`, `lessons.ts`, `exams.ts`, `practical.ts`, `progress.ts`, `index.ts`), with all curriculum and mock datasets decoupled into standalone `.json` files in `src/lib/api/data/` (`courses.json`, `modules.json`, `lessons.json`, `exams.json`, `practical.json`) for 5 courses:
    - **Personal Trainer Level 1** (`personal-trainer-1`): 12 learning modules, 5 workout plans, 12 muscle groups with exercise specs, 30 True/False exam questions.
    - **Personal Trainer Level 2** (`personal-trainer-2`): 13 advanced modules, advanced biomechanics & program design, 30 True/False questions.
    - **Personal Trainer Level 3** (`personal-trainer-3`): 10 master/head coach modules, RFD, 7 case studies, 5 professional documents, project work, 30 True/False questions.
    - **Calisthenics Level 1** (`calisthenics-1`): 13 modules including 178 exercise videos ordered by regression → progression (Push, Pull, Core, Lower Body, Mobility), 30 True/False questions.
    - **Calisthenics Level 2** (`calisthenics-2`): 20 modules (Modules 5-19 dedicated to specific skills: Pull-Up, L-Sit, 180° Bar Rotation, Handstand, Back Lever, Muscle-Up, Dragon Flag, 360° Bar Rotation, HSPU, Human Flag, Front Lever, V-Sit, Dragon 360, Planche, One-Arm Pull-Up), 30 True/False questions.
  - Implemented dynamic App Router routes:
    - `src/app/[locale]/academy/corsi/[slug]/page.tsx`: Dynamic public course detail page with facts, pricing, syllabus, and direct LMS access.
    - `src/app/[locale]/academy/corsi/[slug]/learn/page.tsx`: Course LMS home with progress bar, module cards (Not Started / In Progress / Completed), practical resources, and final exam card.
    - `src/app/[locale]/academy/corsi/[slug]/learn/[moduleId]/page.tsx`: Lesson player with video container, PDF study guides (open online / download), takeaways, technical exercise specifications (target muscle, equipment, setup, execution, safety points, common mistakes), mark-as-complete toggle, and adjacent lesson navigation.
  - Created reusable UI components in `src/components/features/courses/`:
    - `CourseHome.tsx`, `CourseDetailDynamic.tsx`, `CourseCompletion.tsx` (celebration screen with Libertas EPS CONI diploma issuance details), `ExamPlaceholder.tsx` (interactive 30 T/F questions with counter, progress bar, instant feedback, and scoring), `LessonView.tsx`, `LessonNav.tsx`, `ModuleCard.tsx`, `PracticalResources.tsx` (workout plans & 1:1 muscle group explorer), `ProgressBar.tsx`, `StatusBadge.tsx`.
    - Scoped CSS modules `course-home.module.css` and `lesson-view.module.css` strictly adhering to project design tokens.
  - Updated `CourseCatalog.tsx` to feature all 5 distinct courses with dynamic routing.
  - Updated `CourseTabs.tsx` to dynamically render course-specific syllabus modules and FAQs.
  - Added bilingual English dictionary mappings in `src/lib/translations.ts` for all LMS titles, badges, and labels.
  - Redirected legacy `personal-trainer` route to `personal-trainer-1` for backward compatibility.
  - Documented architectural decisions in `brain/decisions.md` (ADR-021) and updated route tree in `brain/routing_and_navigation.md`.
